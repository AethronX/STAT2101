B34-3

Branches: bus 0.6 → late 0.10 / on time 0.90; not bus 0.4 → late 0.04 / on time 0.96.

(a) Multiplication rule 2: 0.6 × 0.10 = 0.06 (b) add both late paths: 0.06 + 0.4 × 0.04 = 0.06 + 0.016 = 0.076 (c) 0.4 × 0.96 = 0.384

"Of the bus users" makes the 10% a conditional probability, P(late | bus).
