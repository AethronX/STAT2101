Counting Rules قواعد العدّ

Everything so far needed n(E) and n(S). When the sample space is too large to list, you count it instead. The four main counting rules are: the Fundamental Counting Rule, Factorial Notation, Permutations, and Combinations. This lesson and §4-5 together carry In-Class Quiz II, and §4-5 is where every rule below gets used.

1 · The Fundamental Counting Rule

Fundamental counting rule

In a sequence of n events in which the first has k1 possibilities, the second has k2, the third has k3, and so on, the total number of possibilities of the sequence is

k1 × k2 × ⋯ × kn

الشرح بالعربي — قاعدة العدّ الأساسية

إذا كان لديك سلسلة من الاختيارات — الأول له k₁ طريقة، والثاني k₂، والثالث k₃... — فعدد كل الاحتمالات = حاصل ضربها.

مثال: 7 ألوان × 2 نوع × 3 ملمس × 2 استخدام = 84 نوع دهان.

  • مع التكرار (repetition allowed): الأرقام تبقى كما هي من اليسار لليمين ← 10 × 10 × 10 × 10 = 10,000.
  • بدون تكرار (not allowed): الأرقام تنقص واحدًا في كل خانة ← 10 × 9 × 8 × 7 = 5,040.
Example 4-38 · Tossing a Coin and Rolling a Die

A coin is tossed and a die is rolled. Find the number of outcomes for the sequence of events.

2 × 6 = 12 outcomes

Namely H1, H2, H3, H4, H5, H6, T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, T6.

Example 4-39 · Types of Paint

A paint manufacturer wishes to manufacture several different paints. The categories include — Color: red, blue, white, black, green, brown, yellow. Type: latex, oil. Texture: flat, semigloss, high gloss. Use: outdoor, indoor. How many different kinds of paint can be made if you select one color, one type, one texture, and one use?

Count the options in each category: 7 colors, 2 types, 3 textures, 2 uses.

7 × 2 × 3 × 2 = 84 different kinds of paint

Example 4-40 · Distribution of Blood Types

There are four blood types: A, B, AB, O. Blood can also be Rh+ or Rh−. A blood donor can be classified as either male or female. How many different ways can a donor have his or her blood labeled?

4 × 2 × 2 = 16 ways

Example 4-41 · Railroad Memorial License Plates

A license plate has a four-digit identification number. How many different plates can be made if: (a) digits are from 0 to 9 and repetitions are permitted? (b) digits are from 0 to 9 and repetitions are not permitted?

  1. (a) With repetition. Every slot still has all 10 digits available:
    10 × 10 × 10 × 10 = 104 = 10,000 plates
  2. (b) Without repetition. Each digit used is removed from the pool:
    10 × 9 × 8 × 7 = 5,040 plates
The lecturer's own rule of thumb

"If repetitions are permitted, then the numbers stay the same going from left to right. If repetitions are not permitted, then the numbers decrease by 1 for each place left to right." Write this on your formula sheet exactly as stated — it answers half of all counting-rule questions on sight.

2 · Factorial notation

Factorial

n! = n × (n − 1) × (n − 2) × ⋯ × (2 × 1)

0! = 1

Examples: 5! = 5 · 4 · 3 · 2 · 1 = 120  ·  9! = 9 · 8 · 7 · 6 · 5 · 4 · 3 · 2 · 1 = 362,880

الشرح بالعربي — المضروب

المضروب n! (يُقرأ n factorial) هو حاصل ضرب كل الأعداد من n نزولًا إلى 1، مثل 5! = 5×4×3×2×1 = 120. وهو يساوي عدد طرق ترتيب n أشياء مختلفة في صف.

تعريف مهم جدًا: 0! = 1 وليس صفرًا.

Factorials worth knowing cold
n012345678910
n!1126241207205,04040,320362,8803,628,800

Casio keystrokes. To compute (5 + 3)! = 40,320: ( 5 + 3 )  SHIFT  x−1 (x!)  =

Trap — 0! = 1, not 0

This matters because nPn = n!/(n−n)! = n!/0! = n! and nCn = n!/(0! · n!) = 1. If you set 0! = 0 you get a division by zero and lose the whole question. Memorise it as a definition, not as a calculation.

3 · Permutations — order matters

A permutation is an arrangement of n objects in a specific order.

Permutation Rule 1 — n objects taken r at a time

nPr = n!(n − r)!

Casio: to compute 7P4 → 7  SHIFT  × (nPr)  4  =

الشرح بالعربي — التباديل (الترتيب مهم)

nPr = عدد طرق اختيار r من n مع ترتيبها. نستخدمها عندما يكون الترتيب مهمًا.

علامات أن الترتيب مهم: ترتيب أو تصنيف (rank)، مراكز (أول/ثاني/ثالث)، مناصب مختلفة (رئيس/نائب/أمين سر)، ترتيب الظهور، خانات مسمّاة (خريف/ربيع)، أرقام كود أو لوحة.

مثال: اختيار 3 ضيوف من 6 مع أهمية ترتيب ظهورهم = 6P3 = 6!/3! = 120.

Example 4-43 · Business Location

(a) A business owner has a choice of 5 locations in which to establish her business. She decides to rank each location according to certain criteria, such as price of the store and parking facilities. How many different ways can she rank the 5 locations?
(b) Suppose she wishes to rank only the top 3 locations selected from 5 locations. How many different ways can she rank them?

  1. (a) All 5 ranked, order matters: 5! = 5 · 4 · 3 · 2 · 1 = 120 ways
    Equivalently: 5P5 = 5!/0! = 120/1 = 120
  2. (b) 3 chosen from 5, order matters: 5P3 = 5!(5 − 3)! = 1202 = 60 ways
Example 4-44 · Radio Show Guests

A radio talk show host can select 3 of 6 special guests for her program. The order of appearance of the guests is important. How many different ways can this be done?

"Order of appearance is important" → permutation.

6P3 = 6!(6 − 3)! = 7206 = 120 ways

Example 4-45 · School Musical Plays

A school musical director can select 2 musical plays to present next year. One will be presented in the fall, and one will be presented in the spring. If she has 9 to pick from, how many different possibilities are there?

The two slots are distinguishable (fall vs spring), so order matters → permutation.

9P2 = 9!(9 − 2)! = 362,8805,040 = 72 possibilities

Why not a combination? Play X in fall + play Y in spring is a different programme from Y in fall + X in spring. Labelled slots always mean permutation.

Example 4-42 · Lock Codes

A person needs to make up a four-digit combination to open the lock on the garage door. The person decides to use four different digits from the first four digits, 0 through 3. How many different combinations can be made?

Four distinct digits arranged in all four positions — a full permutation:

4! = 4 · 3 · 2 · 1 = 24 combinations

Note the vocabulary trap: everyday English calls this a "combination lock", but mathematically it is a permutation — 0123 and 3210 open different locks. The word "combination" in a question is never evidence; only the question "does order matter?" decides.

Permutation Rule 2 — when some objects are identical

nPr₁ r₂ r₃ … rp = n!r1! · r2! · r3! ⋯ rp!

where r1 + r2 + ⋯ + rp = n. Divide out the arrangements you cannot tell apart.

الشرح بالعربي — تباديل مع عناصر متكررة

إذا كانت بعض الأشياء متطابقة (مثل الحروف المكررة في كلمة)، نقسم n! على مضروب عدد تكرار كل عنصر، لأن تبديل عنصرين متطابقين لا يعطي ترتيبًا جديدًا.

مثال: كلمة STATISTICS فيها 10 حروف: S ثلاث مرات، T ثلاث مرات، I مرتان ← 10! ÷ (3! × 3! × 2!) = 50,400.

نصيحة: عدّ الحروف أولًا وتأكد أن مجموع التكرارات يساوي n.

Example 4-46 · Letter Permutations

How many permutations of the letters can be made from the word STATISTICS?

  1. Count the letters: S-T-A-T-I-S-T-I-C-S → n = 10.
  2. Tally repeats: S appears 3 times, T appears 3 times, I appears 2 times, A once, C once. Check: 3 + 3 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 10 ✔
  3. Permutations = 10!3! · 3! · 2! · 1! · 1! = 3,628,8006 · 6 · 2 = 3,628,80072 = 50,400

How many different ways can 5 identical tubes of tartar control toothpaste, 3 identical tubes of bright white toothpaste, and 4 identical tubes of mint toothpaste be arranged in a grocery store counter display?

  1. n = 5 + 3 + 4 = 12 tubes; three identical groups of sizes 5, 3, 4.

4 · Combinations — order does not matter

A selection of distinct objects without regard to order is called a combination.

Combination rule

nCr = n!(n − r)! · r!

Important: in a combination, the order or arrangement of the objects is not important; order is important in a permutation. Casio: to compute 10C4 → 10  SHIFT  ÷ (nCr)  4  =

الشرح بالعربي — التوافيق (الترتيب غير مهم)

nCr = عدد طرق اختيار r من n بدون ترتيب — مجرد اختيار.

علامات أن الترتيب غير مهم: لجنة بدون مناصب، فريق، مجموعة، اختيار كتب أو أفلام أو مواضيع، سحب عدة قطع من صندوق دفعة واحدة.

العلاقة: nCr = nPr ÷ r!، لذلك التوافيق دائمًا أصغر من أو تساوي التباديل.

مثال: اختيار 4 أفلام من 10 = 10C4 = 210. وإذا كان الاختيار من مجموعتين (3 نساء من 7 و رجلان من 5) فإننا نضرب: 7C3 × 5C2 = 35 × 10 = 350.

The relationship worth seeing once

nCr = nPr ÷ r! — a combination is a permutation with the r! internal orderings divided out. That is why nCr is always ≤ nPr. If your combination answer comes out larger than the matching permutation, you have used the wrong formula.

Example 4-48 · Movies at the Park

The director of Movies at the Park must select 4 movies from a total of 10 movies to show on Movie Night at the Park. How many different ways can the selections be made?

Just a selection — nothing says the four are shown in a particular order → combination.

10C4 = 10!(10 − 4)! · 4! = 10 · 9 · 8 · 74 · 3 · 2 · 1 = 5,04024 = 210 ways

Example 4-49 · Committee Selection

In a club there are 7 women and 5 men. A committee of 3 women and 2 men is to be chosen. How many different possibilities are there?

  1. Choose the women: 7C3 = 7!/(4!·3!) = (7·6·5)/(3·2·1) = 210/6 = 35
  2. Choose the men: 5C2 = 5!/(3!·2!) = (5·4)/2 = 10
  3. Combine with the Fundamental Counting Rule (a choice of women and a choice of men):
    35 × 10 = 350 possibilities
Trap — "and" means multiply, not add

Choosing 3 women and 2 men = 35 × 10 = 350. Students who write 35 + 10 = 45 are answering a different question ("choose 3 women or 2 men"). Within counting rules, and → ×, or → +.

5 · Summary of the counting rules (Table 4-1)

Table 4-1 — Summary of counting rules
RuleDefinitionFormula
Fundamental counting ruleThe number of ways a sequence of n events can occur if the first event can occur in k1 ways, the second in k2 ways, etc.k1 · k2 · k3 ⋯ kn
Permutation rule 1The number of permutations of n objects taking r objects at a time (order is important).nPr = n! / (n − r)!
Permutation rule 2The number of permutations of n objects when r1 objects are identical, r2 objects are identical, …, rp objects are identical.n! / (r1! r2! ⋯ rp!)
Combination ruleThe number of combinations of r objects taken from n objects (order is not important).nCr = n! / [(n − r)! r!]
القاعدة الحاسمة: Permutation أم Combination؟ اسأل: هل يتغير الناتج إذا غيّرت الترتيب؟
• نعم (ترتيب، تصنيف، مراكز أول/ثاني، مناصب مختلفة، أرقام لوحة) ← Permutation nPr
• لا (لجنة، مجموعة، اختيار عدد من أشياء بدون أدوار) ← Combination nCr
وتذكّر: كلمة "combination lock" في اللغة الدارجة هي في الحقيقة permutation رياضيًا، فلا تعتمد على الكلمة — اعتمد على السؤال.
