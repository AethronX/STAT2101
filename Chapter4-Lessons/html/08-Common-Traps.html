The 14 traps that cost marks الأخطاء الأربعة عشر التي تضيّع الدرجات

Each of these has a specific home in this chapter. Read the list the night before the exam.

#The trapWhat to do instead
1Confusing mutually exclusive with independent.Mutually exclusive = no overlap (A∩B=∅). Independent = no influence (P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B)). Two different questions, two different tests.
2Using Addition Rule 1 when the events overlap.Always test the intersection first. When in doubt use Rule 2; it reduces to Rule 1 automatically.
3Reading "or" as exclusive.In probability, "A or B" always means A, or B, or both.
4Missing "without replacement".Circle it. It changes every denominator after the first draw and switches you from Rule 1 to Rule 2.
5Putting the wrong thing in the conditional denominator.The condition is the denominator. P(B|A) divides by P(A), never by P(B).
6Treating "of these clients…" / "of those who…" as unconditional.That phrasing always hands you a conditional probability. Annotate it as P(A|H) before you calculate.
7Mixing up "at most" and "less than" (and "at least" / "more than").at → the boundary value is included. than → the boundary value is excluded.
8Doing "at least one" the long way.1 − P(none). Always.
9Using a permutation for a committee.A committee has no internal order → combination. Labelled roles (chair, secretary) → permutation.
10Adding instead of multiplying across categories."3 women and 2 men" → 7C3 × 5C2. "And" is multiplication.
11Writing 0! = 0.0! = 1, by definition. It appears in nPn and nCn.
12Mixing permutations and combinations between n(E) and n(S).Count both with the same rule. Consistency, not cleverness.
13Giving only the final number.Write the sample space in set notation, name the rule you are using, show the substitution. The lecturer states explicitly that set notation is required.
14Reporting a probability greater than 1 or negative.0 ≤ P ≤ 1. If your answer falls outside, you have added when you should have multiplied, or forgotten to divide by n(S). Catch it before you move on.
الأخطاء الأربعة عشر بالعربي
  1. الخلط بين متنافيين (لا تداخل) ومستقلين (لا تأثير).
  2. الجمع المباشر مع أحداث متداخلة — اطرح التقاطع.
  3. فهم or على أنها «أحدهما فقط» — هي «أحدهما أو كلاهما».
  4. تجاهل without replacement — المقام يتغير في السحبة الثانية.
  5. وضع الشيء الخطأ في مقام الاحتمال الشرطي — الشرط هو المقام.
  6. اعتبار of those who احتمالًا عاديًا — هو احتمال شرطي.
  7. الخلط بين at most و less than — كلمة at تعني أن الرقم داخل الحساب.
  8. حساب at least one بالطريقة الطويلة — استخدم 1 − P(none).
  9. استخدام التباديل للجان — اللجنة بدون مناصب = توافيق.
  10. الجمع بدل الضرب عند الاختيار من فئتين — and تعني ضرب.
  11. كتابة 0! = 0 — الصحيح 0! = 1.
  12. خلط التباديل والتوافيق بين البسط والمقام.
  13. كتابة الناتج النهائي فقط — اكتب فضاء العينة واسم القانون والتعويض.
  14. احتمال أكبر من 1 أو سالب — يعني أن في الحل خطأ؛ راجعه قبل أن تنتقل.
