Part B · Short exam problems by lesson

Classification with justification, Table D, systematic k, find-the-mistake.

B1
1-1 CLO1-A

To study daily study hours of all 3,500 first-year SQU students, a researcher asks 150 randomly chosen first-year students; their mean is 2.4 hours. Identify (a) the population and N, (b) the sample and n, (c) the variable with its type and level, (d) whether 2.4 is a parameter or a statistic, (e) the branch of statistics if the researcher concludes "first-year students study about 2.4 hours a day".

B2
1-1 CLO1-A

Census or survey? Justify each. (a) The NCSI collects data on every person, household and building in Oman. (b) SQU emails a questionnaire to 300 randomly chosen students. (c) A teacher records the marks of every student in her class in order to describe that class.

B3
1-2 CLO1-B

Give the type (qualitative or quantitative, discrete or continuous) and the level of measurement of each: (a) governorate of residence (b) number of siblings (c) body temperature in °C (d) customer rating poor / fair / good / excellent (e) time to finish an exam, in minutes (f) Oman Air flight number WY101.

B4
1-2 CLO1-B

Find the boundaries of each recorded value: (a) 48 kg (b) GPA 3.7 (c) 0.125 s.

B5
1-2 CLO1-B · CLO3-C

"Muscat's temperature rose from 20 °C in January to 40 °C in June, so June is twice as hot." Evaluate the claim.

B6
1-2 CLO1-B

Two students describe "number of goals scored in a match". Student A: "quantitative, continuous, because it can be large." Student B: "quantitative, discrete, ratio." Who is right, and what is wrong with the other answer?

B7
1-3 CLO1-C

A company has N = 750 employees numbered 001–750. Select a simple random sample of n = 6 using Table D, starting at row 2, column 1 (06512 07836 88456 36313 30879 51323 76451 25578 15986 50845 57015 53684 57054 93261).

B8
1-3 CLO1-C

A list of 250 patients is to be sampled systematically, n = 12. (a) Find k and list the sample if the random start is 7. (b) A student rounds k up to 21 and the random start happens to be 21. What goes wrong?

B9
1-3 CLO1-C · CLO3-C

A researcher wants to compare study habits of male and female SQU students, but females are about two-thirds of the students. Explain why a stratified sample is better than a simple random sample here, and describe how to take it.

B10
1-3 CLO1-C

Name the sampling method and say whether it is likely to be biased: (a) the first 30 shoppers to enter Lulu on Saturday morning (b) an online poll on a news website that anyone can answer (c) the names of all 2,000 employees are placed in a box and 40 are drawn.

B11
1-3 CLO1-C

The Ministry of Education wants grade-10 maths scores. Plan P: randomly pick 10 of the 120 schools and test every grade-10 student in them. Plan Q: in every one of the 120 schools, randomly pick 5 grade-10 students. Name each plan and give one advantage of each.

B12
1-3 CLO1-C

Find the mistake. For 86 trees and n = 6, a student used k = 15 and a random start of 13: "13, 28, 43, 58, 73, 88".

B13
1-3 CLO1-C

Find the mistake. For N = 4998 laptops, a student read Table D row 2 in 5-digit groups and chose laptops 06512 and 07836 first.

B14
1-1 1-3 CLO1-A · CLO1-C · CLO3-C

A website surveys 50 members of a "sleep well" club, who volunteered, and concludes: "Students who sleep more get higher GPAs." (a) Which branch of statistics is the conclusion? (b) Which sampling method was used? (c) Give two reasons to doubt the conclusion.

Part C · Long structured questions

Midterm-style, multi-part. Marks in brackets.

C1
1-2 CLO1-B [9 marks]

An SQU hospital record contains: (a) name (b) age in years (c) weight in kg (d) condition: critical / serious / fair / good (e) date of admission (f) number of previous visits (g) ward number (h) body temperature in °C (i) blood type. For each, state qualitative or quantitative (and discrete or continuous) and the level of measurement.

C2
1-1 1-2 1-3 CLO1-A · B · C [10 marks]

The Oman Traffic Authority wants to know the average daily driving time of licensed drivers in Oman. It plans to ask a sample of 1,200 drivers.

  1. State the population, the sample and the variable, with the variable's type and level.
  2. Describe a stratified design and a cluster design for this study.
  3. Give one possible nonsampling error.
  4. Classify: (i) "The 1,200 drivers averaged 74 minutes a day." (ii) "Omani drivers spend between 70 and 78 minutes a day driving."
C3
1-3 CLO1-C [10 marks]

A college lists its 480 students, numbered 001–480. Table D row 2: 06512 07836 88456 36313 30879 51323 76451 25578 15986 50845 57015 53684 57054 93261.

  1. Select a simple random sample of 8 students starting at row 2, column 1.
  2. Select a systematic sample of 8: find k, and take the random start as the first 2-digit number in row 2 that is between 01 and k.
  3. The college wants both male and female students represented. Which method should it use, and how?
C4
1-2 CLO1-B · CLO3-C [8 marks]

A survey records: (i) gender, (ii) satisfaction coded 1–5, (iii) room temperature in °C, (iv) monthly income in OMR. For each variable, state the level and say whether each comparison is meaningful: "is different from", "is greater than", "is 10 units more than", "is twice".
