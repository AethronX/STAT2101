How Chapter 1 is actually examined

Almost nothing here is calculation. The marks come from classification under ambiguity: a scenario is described and you name the branch (descriptive or inferential), the variable type, the level of measurement, or the sampling method, and justify it in one line. The questions are built around the borderline cases: a numeric ID (qualitative), money (discrete in Bluman), calendar year (interval, not ratio), "all teachers from two buildings" (cluster, not stratified). Each lesson here ends its main ideas with those borderline cases. The two calculations are the random-number-table SRS and the systematic sampling interval k.
