2 · Discrete vs continuous Both · CLO1-B

DiscreteمنفصلContinuousمتصل
DescriptionCountable values (0, 1, 2, 3, …), no fractions or decimals; obtained by counting.Infinitely many values between any two values; fractions and decimals; obtained by measuring.
Exampleschildren in a family; students in a classroom; calls a call centre receives each day; touchdowns scored per season; pens in a boxtemperature, weight, GPA, age, height, volume, area, distance, exam marks, air-pollution concentration, time
Continuous data are recorded rounded, so a value stands for an interval (Table 1-1) Ahmed
VariableRecordedBoundaries
Length15 cm14.5–15.5 cm

Rule: subtract and add half of one unit of the last recorded digit. Whole numbers ± 0.5; one decimal ± 0.05; two decimals ± 0.005. The same rule builds class boundaries in Chapter 2.

Example 1-2 · Discrete or continuous? Ahmed

(a) Hours per week children aged 12–15 reported watching TV. (b) Touchdowns a quarterback scored each year. (c) Money a person earns per week at a fast-food restaurant. (d) Weights of NFL players.

(a) ⦃continuous⦄: time is measured. (b) ⦃discrete⦄: counted. (c) ⦃discrete⦄: in Bluman, money changes in steps of the smallest unit (cents, or baisa in Oman), so it is countable. (d) ⦃continuous⦄: measured.

3 · The four levels of measurement Both · CLO1-B

LevelDefinitionExamples (both decks + Table 1-2)
Nominal (lowest)اسميData are classified into mutually exclusive (nonoverlapping) categories with no natural order. Numbers, if used, are just identifiers. No arithmetic; differences are meaningless.gender, hair colour, phone brand, days of the week, months, names, nationality, student ID, zip code, eye colour, political or religious affiliation, major field, blood type
OrdinalترتيبيCategories that can be ranked (natural order), but precise differences between ranks do not exist or are not meaningful (the gap A–B may differ from B–C; two students with grade A can have different scores).letter grades, superior/average/poor, health status, level of education, Likert scale (strongly agree … strongly disagree), judging 1st/2nd/3rd, ranking of tennis players, rating poor/good/excellent
IntervalفتريRanked, with precise, meaningful differences, but no true zero: zero does not mean absence. Addition and subtraction are valid; ratios are not (140 IQ is not "twice as intelligent" as 70).temperature in °C or °F, IQ, SAT score, dates (production/expiry, birth date), years (graduation 2018), exact clock time (leaving home at 8:35 AM)
Ratio (highest)نسبيAll interval properties plus a true zero (0 means none). All arithmetic is valid, ratios included: 20 kg is twice 10 kg.height, weight, area, age, salary or income, number of phone calls, exam marks, GPA, time duration (45 minutes), temperature in Kelvin

Borderline cases examiners use

Year / date / clock time: interval, but time duration (45 minutes) is ratio. Temperature: °C and °F are interval, Kelvin is ratio. Numeric codes (ID, zip code, phone number): nominal and qualitative. Exam marks: ratio in Dr. Ahmed's list, but letter grades are ordinal. IQ and SAT: interval.
