The variance is an average of squared deviations, so it is never negative, however the values sit relative to the mean.

A123-3 CLO3-C

A133-3 CLO3-C

A143-3 CLO1-A

A153-3 CLO3-C

Part D · True / false with justification, and find the mistake

A one-word answer earns nothing. The justification is the mark.

D13-2 CLO1-A · CLO3-C

True. s = 0 means Σ(X − x̄)² = 0, so every deviation is 0 and every value equals the mean.

D23-1 CLO1-A · CLO3-C

False. For even n it is the mean of the two middle values: the median of 2, 4, 6, 8 is 5.

D33-2 CLO1-A · CLO3-C

True. The SD is unchanged but the mean shifts, so s/x̄ changes.

D43-2 CLO1-A · CLO3-C

False. It guarantees at least 75%; the actual percentage is often higher.

D53-4 CLO1-A · CLO3-C
