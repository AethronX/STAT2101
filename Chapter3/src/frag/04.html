2 · Five-number summary and boxplot Both

A data set is summarised by five values, reported in this order: (1) minimum; (2) Q₁ = P₂₅; (3) Q₂ = median = P₅₀; (4) Q₃ = P₇₅; (5) maximum.

Constructing a boxplot Amani: Step 1 find the five-number summary. Step 2 draw a horizontal axis with a scale that includes the minimum and maximum. Step 3 draw a box whose vertical sides pass through Q₁ and Q₃, with a vertical line through the median. Step 4 draw a line (whisker) from the minimum to the left side of the box and from the maximum to the right side.

The appearance of a boxplot changes with skewness, but its structure stays the same.

Simple example Amani

7, 15, 36, 39, 42, 43, 46, 49, 50, 51, 52, 61, 69, 80, 90. Construct a boxplot.

n = 15 (odd), so the median is the 8th value, ⦃49⦄. Lower seven: 7 15 36 39 42 43 46, so Q₁ = ⦃39⦄. Upper seven: 50 51 52 61 69 80 90, so Q₃ = ⦃61⦄.

Five-number summary: 7, 39, 49, 61, 90.

Activity Amani

Construct a boxplot for 12, 5, 22, 30, 7, 36, 14, 42, 15, 53, 25.

Ordered: 5 7 12 14 15 22 25 30 36 42 53 (n = 11). MD = ⦃22⦄. Q₁ = median of 5 7 12 14 15 = ⦃12⦄. Q₃ = median of 25 30 36 42 53 = ⦃36⦄.

Five-number summary: 5, 12, 22, 36, 53. IQR = 24 and the fences are −24 and 72, so there are no outliers.

4 · Side-by-side boxplots Both · CLO3-C

Ex 3-38 · Roller coaster speeds (mph) Hadhrami

Wood: 50 56 60 48 35 67 72 68 · Steel: 55 70 48 28 100 106 102 120. Compare the distributions using boxplots.

Wood ordered: 35 48 50 56 | 60 67 68 72, giving five-number summary 35, 49, 58, 67.5, 72. Steel ordered: 28 48 55 70 | 100 102 106 120, giving 28, 51.5, 85, 104, 120.

(1) The median of the steel coasters is much higher (85 vs 58). (2) The IQR (spread) of the steel coasters is much larger (52.5 vs 18.5). (3) The range of the steel coasters is larger (92 vs 37). (4) The steel coasters are negatively skewed (median nearer Q₃); the wooden coasters are about symmetric.

Comparing two classes' grades Amani

The same four-point comparison: Class B's median is higher (it performed better overall); Class B's IQR is larger (grades more spread out); its range is wider (more variation in performance); Class B is left-skewed (some students scored much lower than the median) while Class A is right-skewed. Use this template, centre → spread (IQR) → range → shape, for every side-by-side question.

المخطط الصندوقي يُرسم من الملخص الخماسي: أصغر قيمة، ⟦Q₁⟧، الوسيط، ⟦Q₃⟧، أكبر قيمة. وكل جزء من أجزائه الأربعة يحتوي 25% من البيانات مهما كان طوله، فالجزء الأطول لا يعني بيانات أكثر بل تشتتًا أكبر. لمعرفة الشكل: إذا كان الوسيط يسار منتصف الصندوق والخط الأيمن أطول ← ملتوٍ لليمين (موجب)، والعكس ← ملتوٍ لليسار (سالب). وعند المقارنة بين مجموعتين اكتب أربع نقاط بالترتيب: (1) الوسيط، (2) المدى الربيعي، (3) المدى، (4) الشكل. ودعّم كل نقطة بالأرقام.
R for Lab Quiz I (outline CLO3-D)

fivenum(x) gives the five-number summary, boxplot(x, horizontal = TRUE) draws the boxplot, and boxplot(wood, steel, names = c("Wood", "Steel"), horizontal = TRUE) draws side-by-side boxplots. R's boxplot whiskers stop at the last value inside the 1.5·IQR fences and draw any outliers as separate points. fivenum() can differ from the hand method when n is odd: for Dr. Amani's 15 values it gives hinges 40.5 and 56.5 instead of Q₁ = 39 and Q₃ = 61.
